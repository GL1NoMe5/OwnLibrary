## Rain at a window

- Collection: Rain (loopable)
- Author: Ylmir
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Rain%20OGG.zip
- Original archive member: 3.ogg
- Bundled file: src/renderer/src/assets/audio/rain-window.ogg
- Recorded by the author with a mono microphone at a window and processed for stereo.

The bundled files preserve the downloaded audio. At playback the app trims 80 ms
from each end, joins the ends with a 1.5-second equal-power crossfade, and balances
the level of the two layers. No network access or sound-bank account is needed.
These recordings are not covered by the application's code license.
